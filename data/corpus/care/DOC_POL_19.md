## §1 Eligibility

Customers who identify a disability that makes being without the device materially
harder are moved to the front of the walk-in queue and prioritised for loaner allocation
under DOC_POL_15 §1, irrespective of whether they hold an extended plan.

## §2 Evidence

No documentation is required. The customer's statement is sufficient and must not be
questioned at the counter.
