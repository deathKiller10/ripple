## §1 Revised charging basis

With effect from 1 May 2026 this section replaces DOC_POL_21 §1. Accessories replaced
outside their coverage period are charged at the accessory list price with no handling
fee. The two hundred rupee handling fee is withdrawn entirely. Ordering lead times for
non-stocked accessories are unchanged at five working days.

## §2 Scope of the amendment

The bundling waiver in DOC_POL_21 §2 is now redundant and is withdrawn with it. No other
provision of DOC_POL_21 is affected.
