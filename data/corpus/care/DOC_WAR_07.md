title: Coverage for refurbished and exchange units
type: warranty
---

## §1 Coverage period

A refurbished device supplied as a warranty exchange carries the remainder of the
original device's warranty or ninety days, whichever is longer. A refurbished device
purchased outright carries a full twelve-month warranty from the date of that purchase.

## §2 Identification

Exchange units are identified by a serial number prefix recorded against the original
service request. Where a customer disputes that a device is refurbished, the service
request history is authoritative.
