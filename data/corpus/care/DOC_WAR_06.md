## §1 Definition

A device that fails within seven days of purchase with a confirmed hardware fault is
treated as dead on arrival. Early-life failure covers the period from eight to thirty
days.

## §2 Handling

Dead on arrival devices are returned to the point of sale for exchange, not repaired at
a service centre. The service centre issues a technical confirmation report that the
retailer requires before exchanging. Between eight and thirty days the device is
repaired under the standard warranty unless the retailer's own return policy is more
favourable.

## §3 Exclusions

Physical or liquid damage found on inspection removes the device from the dead-on-
arrival process entirely, whatever the age. The finding is recorded with photographs
before the device is returned.
