## §1 When a revision is permitted

Where a second fault is found after an approved repair has started, work stops and a
revised quotation is issued. The customer may approve the revision, decline the
additional work, or withdraw the device with only the originally approved work
completed.

## §2 What may not be revised

The labour tier quoted at approval is fixed. A revision may add parts but may not
reprice labour for work already approved, even where the repair proves more difficult
than expected.
