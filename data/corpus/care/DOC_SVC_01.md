## §1 Channels

Appointments are booked through Samsung Members, the support website or by calling the
contact centre. Walk-in service is available at all authorised centres but is served
after booked appointments and may involve a wait.

## §2 What the customer must bring

The device, the proof of purchase required by DOC_WAR_01 §4, and a government photograph
identity document. For an imported device the additional import evidence in DOC_WAR_03
§3 is also required at the time of booking, not at collection.
