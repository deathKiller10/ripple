## §1 When refurbished parts are used

Refurbished parts may be used for out-of-warranty repairs where a new part is
unavailable or where the customer elects the lower price. The customer must be told
before work begins and the quotation must state it explicitly.

## §2 Warranty on the repair

A repair using a refurbished part carries a ninety-day warranty on that part, the same
as a repair using a new part. The device's underlying warranty status is unchanged by
the choice.
