## §1 Customer obligation

Customers are responsible for backing up their data before handing over a device.
Service centres are not able to recover data from a device that fails during repair and
offer no data recovery service.

## §2 Account removal

The customer must remove their account and disable device protection before the device
is accepted. A device that arrives with device protection active cannot be tested after
repair and will be returned unrepaired.
