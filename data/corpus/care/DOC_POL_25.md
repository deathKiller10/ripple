title: Recording and disclosing liquid damage findings
type: policy
---

## §1 Procedure

The liquid damage indicator is checked on every device presented with an electrical
fault and the result photographed before disassembly. The photograph is attached to the
service request.

## §2 Disclosure

Where the indicator is triggered, the customer is shown the photograph and told that
DOC_WAR_05 §1 applies before any quotation is discussed. A customer who disputes the
finding in writing has the case referred to the regional desk under DOC_SVC_04 §2.
