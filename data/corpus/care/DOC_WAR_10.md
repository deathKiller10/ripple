## §1 Scope

Devices purchased on a business account and deployed as a fleet are covered by the
standard limited warranty unless a separate enterprise agreement applies. Where one
does, its terms override this document and the account manager must be consulted before
quoting.

## §2 Bulk handling

Fleet repairs are booked as a batch against the business account rather than
individually. Turnaround commitments in DOC_POL_11 apply per device, not per batch.
