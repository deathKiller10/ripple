## §1 Symptom

A persistent vertical band, usually green but occasionally pink, appearing down one side
of the display. The band is present from boot and does not change with brightness or
content. This is a panel-level defect and is distinct from the intermittent flicker
described in DOC_KB_01.

## §2 Handling

No software remediation exists. Book a display assembly replacement directly. Where the
device is within the standard warranty period and shows no physical or liquid damage,
the replacement is covered. Devices with an extended protection plan follow the terms in
DOC_WAR_04.
