## §1 Part numbers

Galaxy S24 battery pack EB-BS921ABY, Galaxy S24 Plus EB-BS926ABY, Galaxy S24 Ultra EB-
BS928ABY. Battery replacement requires display removal on all three models and is
therefore quoted with display-tier labour under DOC_POL_09 §1.

## §2 List prices

Indicative list prices excluding labour are three thousand four hundred rupees for the
S24 pack and three thousand nine hundred rupees for the Plus and Ultra packs. Batteries
replaced under warranty against the degradation threshold carry no parts or labour
charge.
