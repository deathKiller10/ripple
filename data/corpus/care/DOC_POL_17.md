## §1 Coverage

Every completed repair carries a ninety-day warranty covering the parts replaced and the
workmanship. A recurrence of the same fault within that period is repaired at no charge,
including labour.

## §2 What is not covered

A different fault, or the same fault arising from new physical damage, is a new repair
and is quoted normally. Where there is disagreement about whether a fault is a
recurrence, the original technician's notes and the diagnostic codes on both visits
decide it.
