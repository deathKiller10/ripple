## §1 Availability

Doorstep pickup is available in selected metropolitan pin codes for in-warranty repairs
on flagship devices. The service adds two working days to the turnaround commitments in
DOC_POL_11 to allow for collection and return transit.

## §2 Exclusions

Doorstep pickup is not offered for out-of-warranty repairs, for devices with suspected
liquid damage, or for imported devices serviced under DOC_WAR_03. Customers in these
categories must attend a service centre.
