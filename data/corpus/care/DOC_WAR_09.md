## §1 Transferability

The standard limited warranty follows the device, not the purchaser, and runs from the
date of first retail sale. A second-hand buyer is covered for whatever remains, provided
the original tax invoice is available.

## §2 Extended plans

Extended protection plans are not transferable and terminate on resale. This differs
from the standard warranty and is a frequent source of confusion; state it explicitly
before quoting any repair.
