title: Explaining a declined warranty claim
type: service
---

## §1 What must be said

A declined claim is explained with the specific clause relied on, the diagnostic
evidence, and the chargeable alternative with a price. A decline communicated without a
clause reference is not complete and will be overturned on escalation.

## §2 Customer options

The customer may accept the chargeable repair, decline and pay the diagnostic fee under
DOC_POL_07 §2, or escalate under DOC_SVC_04. All three must be stated; offering only the
first is a process failure.
