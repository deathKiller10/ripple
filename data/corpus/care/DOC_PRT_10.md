## §1 Compatibility

Certified 25-watt and 45-watt adapters are interchangeable across the S24 family; the
device negotiates the rate it supports. A 45-watt adapter does not damage a device that
supports only 25 watts. Cables must be rated 5A for charging above 25 watts.

## §2 List prices

Indicative list prices are one thousand six hundred rupees for the 45-watt adapter, one
thousand two hundred for the 25-watt, and four hundred fifty for a 5A cable. Charging
accessories replaced outside coverage are charged under DOC_POL_23 §1.
