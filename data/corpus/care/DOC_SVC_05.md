type: service

## §1 Process

Appointments can be changed or cancelled through the same channel used to book, up to
two hours before the slot. Inside two hours the slot is released to walk-ins and the
customer must rebook.

## §2 Repeated no-shows

Three no-shows within ninety days restricts the account to walk-in service for the
following ninety days. The customer is told when the restriction is applied, not
afterwards.
