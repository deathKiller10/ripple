## §1 Contents

A technical confirmation report states the device model, serial number, the fault
reproduced, the diagnostic codes recorded, and whether the fault is a manufacturing
defect. It does not state a market value or a recommendation about replacement.

## §2 When it is issued

Issued on request for dead-on-arrival exchanges under DOC_WAR_06 §2, for insurance-
adjacent purposes where a third party requires it, and where a customer is escalating
under DOC_SVC_04. The centre does not correspond with third parties directly.
