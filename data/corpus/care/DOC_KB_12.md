title: Storage full warnings with little user data
type: kb
---

## §1 Symptom

The device reports insufficient storage while the user's own files account for a small
fraction of capacity. Cached application data and orphaned update packages are the usual
cause.

## §2 Remediation

Use Device care to clear cached data. Remove downloaded update packages under Software
update. Where a single application holds several gigabytes of cache, clearing that
application's storage from Settings resolves it without data loss to the user's
documents.
